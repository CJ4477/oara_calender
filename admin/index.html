Use on iOS & Android (PWA + Native Calendar Alarms)

Install on your phone Home Screen & enable 100% reliable background lock-screen reminders

Recommended for Background Lock-Screen Alerts Works Offline on iPhone & Android

1. Sync All OARA Reminders to Apple Calendar (iOS) / Google Calendar (Android)

iOS and Android battery savers pause browser tabs when your phone is locked. Exporting an .ics Calendar Alarm file adds all 105 yearly recurring OARA birthdays & anniversaries directly to your phone's native calendar with an automatic lock-screen alarm at your chosen reminder time (08:00 AM).

🍎

Install on iPhone / iPad (iOS)

  1. Open your deployed app link in Safari on iOS.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add.
  4. Launch OARA Calendar from your Home Screen like a native iOS app!
🤖

Install on Android

  1. Open your deployed app link in Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the Install App banner or the ⋮ (three dots) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Allow notifications when prompted for live reminder alerts!
